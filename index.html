A home base for everything that leaves the ground under its own power.

Ballooning, airships, paramotors, paragliders — the small end of aviation has never had a proper home. No single place for the products, the publications, the services, or the events that bring pilots and pilots-to-be together. Feathermode is building that place, one flight at a time.
