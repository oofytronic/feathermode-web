Aerodash

An everything app in the making: one place for the ways people talk, share and get things done, built on end-to-end encrypted, peer-to-peer and local-first foundations. Communication comes first, because the standard for how people and software talk to each other comes before any feature built on top of it.

  • End-to-end encrypted
  • Peer-to-peer
  • Local-first

Gondola

A distribution platform that lives inside a website or platform you own, and rides inside Aerodash as a mini-app. When walled gardens close the roads to your readers, Gondola carries your work over them. You pay in tokens, because the APIs underneath charge a little per transaction.

  • Self-owned
  • Token-based
  • A mini-app of Aerodash

Named for the cabin slung beneath an airship, which carries the people and the cargo.
