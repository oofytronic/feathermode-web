Common Tongue

An exploration in end-to-end communication through peer-to-peer, local-first and decentralized methods. Common Tongue starts from one idea: the standard for how people and software talk to each other comes before any collaborative app or feature built on top of it.

  • End-to-end encrypted
  • Peer-to-peer
  • Local-first

Named for the shared language everyone agrees on before they can say anything else.

Platform · DistributionIn development

Airlift

A distribution platform that lives inside a website or platform you own. When walled gardens close the roads to your readers, Airlift flies your work over them. You pay in tokens, because the APIs underneath charge a little per transaction.

  • Self-owned
  • Token-based

Named for the airlifts that kept supplies moving when the roads were cut.
